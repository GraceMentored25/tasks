# 📜 AI Contract Risk & Clause Extractor

> **Malt Commercial Reference:** `MALT-AI-CONTRACT-02`
> **Estimated Market Price Range:** `2 000 € - 5 000 €`

## Overview
A fast, privacy-first legal AI MVP that scans contract text, highlights high-risk liabilities, automatically extracts critical clauses (Indemnification, Termination, Governing Law, IP Rights), and calculates a contract risk index.

## Key Features
- **High-Risk Clause Detection:** Pre-cached regex matching to instantly isolate liability caps, auto-renewals, and jurisdiction clauses.
- **Contract Risk Score:** Sub-millisecond calculation of overall risk level (Low, Medium, High, Critical).
- **Zero Latency Parsing:** Processes multi-page legal text locally without external network overhead.
