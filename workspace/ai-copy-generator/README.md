# 📝 AI Copywriting & SEO Readability Scorer

> **Malt Commercial Reference:** `MALT-AI-SEO-03`
> **Estimated Market Price Range:** `1 000 € - 2 500 €`

## Overview
A real-time AI content drafting and SEO optimization assistant. Calculates Flesch-Kincaid readability, target keyword density, reading time, and generates optimized marketing copy variations.

## Key Features
- **Debounced Real-Time Readability Engine:** Instant calculation of sentence length, word count, and Flesch score.
- **Keyword Density Matrix:** Live parsing of targeted keywords with density alerts.
- **AI Copy Generator:** Generates high-converting hero headlines, meta descriptions, and bullet points on demand.
