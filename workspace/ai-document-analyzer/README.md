# AI Document & Contract Analyzer 🛡️ (Commercial MVP)

> **Malt Reference:** [Malt Mission Ref #MT-92104] *Développement d'un Outil IA NLP d'Analyse Automatique de Contrats & Documents Juridiques*
> **TJM Client:** 650€ - 850€ / jour
> **Budget Estimation Projet:** 6 000€ - 9 500€

## Overview
AI Document & Contract Analyzer provides instant clause extraction, liability risk assessment, and compliance summarization for legal contracts and corporate documents using high-speed client-side parsing algorithms.

## Key Features
- ⚡ **O(1) Clause Extraction:** Fast indexing of confidentiality, termination, liability, and payment terms.
- 🎯 **Automated Risk Scoring:** Instant detection of high-risk legal clauses with action points.
- 📋 **Executive Summary Generation:** High-level key takeaways for fast decision-making.
- 📊 **Security & Privacy First:** Client-side zero-leakage analysis logic.
