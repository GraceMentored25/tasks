# ⚡ AI Document & Legal Contract Summarizer

> Commercial AI MVP for Enterprise Legal, HR, and Operations

## 📋 Commercial Reference
- **Platform:** Malt / Freelance AI Marketplace
- **Job Reference:** AI Document & Legal Contract Analyzer
- **Average Market Price:** €2,500 - €5,000

## 🚀 Features & Architecture
- Rapid extraction of key legal clauses (Liability, Termination, Payment terms, Non-Compete).
- Automatic risk rating analysis (Low, Medium, High risk indicator).
- Fast search & keyword highlight in parsed document segments.

## ⚡ Performance Optimizations (Bolt)
- Single-pass regex parsing stream for instant clause detection in large contract texts.
- Memoized clause extraction cache to avoid re-parsing unchanged document buffers.
- DOM node recycling for smooth scrolling through document section lists.
